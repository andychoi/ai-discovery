📈 METRICS YOU GET
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Fitness (0-1%)        | % of logs that replay on discovered model
  Precision (0-1%)      | % of model used by log
  Generalization (0-1%) | Model's ability to handle unseen traces
  
  Bottlenecks           | Activities with high latency or variance
  Edge Frequency        | How often each transition is used
  Cycle Time            | End-to-end process duration

🔐 QUALITY GATES (Configurable)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Default Thresholds:
  - Fitness > 0.90        (90% of traces must replay)
  - Precision > 0.85      (85% of model must be used)
  - Generalization > 0.80 (80% generalization)
  
  If threshold fails:
  - Stage 10.5 continues (non-blocking)
  - fitness_passed = false in DB
  - ⚠️ FAIL badge in report
  - Recommendations provided
